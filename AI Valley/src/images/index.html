How it works

  • Free image model. Images come from the open Flux model on Pollinations, called through a Netlify function so any token stays on the server.
  • Prompt building. Styles and the poster mode turn short inputs into detailed prompts (the poster mode is the old Film Fusion app).
  • Remix and colorize. A free vision LLM writes a detailed description of your photo, including likely colors, and that description is repainted in the chosen style.
