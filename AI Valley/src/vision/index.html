How it works

  • On-device detection. Xenova/yolos-tiny runs on Transformers.js in your browser (WebGPU when available, otherwise WebAssembly). The photo is never uploaded for detection.
  • Vision chat. For questions, the image is shrunk to 1024 px and sent with your question to a free multimodal model on OpenRouter through AI Valley's proxy.
